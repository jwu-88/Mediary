Amoxicillin

Penicillin antibiotic · Prescription only

Rx
Common formCapsule
Strengths250–500 mg
Typical use5–14 days

What it treats

Used to treat certain bacterial infections, including infections of the ear, nose, throat, urinary tract, and skin.

Important safety

  • Do not use with a penicillin allergy
  • Finish the full prescribed course
  • Seek help for swelling or breathing trouble
